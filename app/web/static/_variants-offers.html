1 · Margin note no fill, no rail

The prompt stops being a coloured block and becomes a ruled aside, the way a footnote sits under text. It is the only direction that never outshouts the tester's own prose, because it has no fill to do it with. The three choices separate by weight rather than by colour: one solid button commits, two underlines qualify, and dismissal sits apart on the right. Cheapest vertically — about 46px against the incumbent's 96px.

The most vertical space of the four, and worth it precisely where the decision is irreversible.

4 · Chip silent until asked

This is the direction built for the tester on their ninetieth finding, who already knows what the library holds and wants the page to stop negotiating. The cost is a click before the offer can be acted on, and a chip is easier to never notice.
